Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PulseFlow ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our AI-powered social media scheduling platform hosted at pulseflow.sangitshrestha.com.np.

2. Information We Collect

When you register, connect social accounts, or use PulseFlow, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Credentials: Your name, email address, and securely hashed passwords (using SHA-256).
  • Connected Social Media Data: Account identifiers, profile names, and access tokens for platforms you explicitly connect (such as LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and X) via OAuth authentication.
  • User Content: Prompts, captions, and selected media generated or scheduled through our Workers AI Studio.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to provide, maintain, and improve our services, including:

  • Authenticating your user session and securing your dashboard.
  • Publishing and scheduling social media posts on your behalf to your connected platforms.
  • Generating AI captions and promotional imagery using Cloudflare Workers AI.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Cloudflare D1 databases and Cloudflare Workers infrastructure. API secrets and sensitive credentials are encrypted using Cloudflare environment secrets. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data or social media access tokens to third parties.

5. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You can disconnect your social media accounts at any time directly from your PulseFlow dashboard, which instantly revokes and deletes stored access tokens from our database. You may also request complete account deletion by contacting us.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer administrator at dev@sangitshrestha.com.np.
